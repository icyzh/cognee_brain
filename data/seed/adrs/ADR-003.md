## Context
We are building svc-ledger and the storage layer for svc-payments from scratch. The payment schema is still changing weekly as we add payment methods, and the team wants to ship the first merchants quickly. Marco Rossi has run MongoDB in production before.

## Decision
Store ledger entries and payment records in MongoDB. Each payment is a document with its ledger entries embedded where possible. Balances are computed from entries by the application.

## Alternatives considered
- Postgres: strong transactional guarantees and mature tooling, but schema migrations felt like a drag while the model is still moving.
- DynamoDB: scales well, but the access patterns for reporting and reconciliation are awkward and it locks us into key-based queries.

## Consequences
Fast iteration on the schema. Operations that touch more than one document, such as a refund that updates the payment and writes two ledger entries, rely on application-level care rather than the database. We accept this risk for now and will revisit if we see inconsistencies.
