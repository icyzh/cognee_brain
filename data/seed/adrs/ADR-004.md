## Context
svc-reports builds merchant settlement reports and finance exports by querying the live ledger. Large merchants' month-end reports run long scans that compete with payment writes, and twice in January reports slowed down live captures. Finance also wants SQL access for ad-hoc exports.

## Decision
svc-reports reads only from a Postgres read replica of ledger data, refreshed nightly. Reports are as of the previous day's close. Lena Fischer's Data team owns the replica pipeline and the report jobs.

## Alternatives considered
- Query the primary with lower priority: still risks the payment path and does not give finance SQL.
- A streaming warehouse (change data capture into a warehouse): fresher data, but a lot of infrastructure for a team of two; revisit when merchants ask for intraday reports.

## Consequences
Report load can no longer affect payments. Data in reports is up to 24 hours old, which merchants were told about. The nightly load must finish before the report jobs start, so job timing needs monitoring.
