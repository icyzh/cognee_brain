## Context
svc-notify needs a delivery channel for receipts and refund confirmations. Our current merchants only asked for email receipts, and the team has a single notifications engineer.

## Decision
svc-notify sends customer notifications by email only, through Amazon SES, using templated receipts. Other channels are out of scope for now.

## Alternatives considered
- A multi-channel provider covering email, SMS and push: more capable, but more cost and integration work than current merchants need.
- Running our own SMTP relay: cheaper per message but deliverability and bounce handling would be on us.

## Consequences
One channel keeps svc-notify simple and lets Mei Chen ship receipts quickly on top of the SQS events from ADR-002. Merchants who want SMS or push have to build it themselves. We expect to revisit once larger merchants ask for more channels.
