## Context
This supersedes ADR-005, which limited svc-notify to email through SES. Three of our largest merchants now require SMS receipts and refund alerts, and one wants push notifications through their app. Email open rates for refund confirmations are low and support tickets about "missing" refunds are rising.

## Decision
svc-notify becomes multi-channel: email (SES stays), SMS through an external provider, and push through the merchant's app. Each merchant configures which channels to use per event type. The SQS event flow from ADR-002 is unchanged. Nora Lindqvist owns the decision; Mei Chen builds the channels, starting with SMS (TCK-147).

## Alternatives considered
- Stay email-only and let merchants build SMS themselves: risks losing the three large merchants.
- Replace SES with one provider for all channels: simpler vendor list, but migrating working email gives no benefit now.

## Consequences
Customers get notifications where they read them. SMS adds per-message cost, passed through to merchants. svc-notify needs per-channel retries and a preference store, which adds work for a small team.
