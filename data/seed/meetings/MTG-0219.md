Sam: Recap of Tuesday: a merchant's API key leaked and someone ran card testing against authorise. Thousands of requests a minute.
Arjun: Our per-service limits didn't catch it?
Sam: They're per instance and not tied to the key. We stopped it by revoking the key by hand.
Priya: So we need one place that knows the key and enforces limits.
Sam: The gateway. Per-key limits stored in svc-auth, 100 requests a second by default, lower on authorise. Over the limit returns 429 with Retry-After.
Arjun: Some of the big merchants will hit 100 at peak.
Sam: Then we raise their limits in auth config. That's a one-line change, not a deploy.
Priya: Did we look at a bot-protection vendor?
Sam: Later, maybe. Gateway rules we can ship this week.
Priya: Fine. ADR-006, Sam owns it. Arjun, make sure the SDKs respect Retry-After.
Arjun: On it.
