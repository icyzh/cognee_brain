title: "Auth design review"
attendees: [sam, priya, marco]
decisions: [ADR-001]
---

Sam: Every payment request makes a round trip to svc-auth just to check the token. If auth is down, payments are down.
Marco: We saw that last week. Auth had a slow deploy and capture latency doubled.
Priya: What's the proposal?
Sam: Signed JWTs. Services verify locally with the public keys, no call to auth on the hot path.
Marco: What about revocation? That's the usual problem with JWTs.
Sam: Short expiry. Fifteen minutes on the access token, and a refresh token we keep server-side and can revoke.
Priya: Why not a day? Fewer refreshes.
Sam: A stolen token valid for a day on a payments API is too much. Fifteen minutes caps the damage.
Marco: Fine by me, as long as the SDKs handle refresh transparently.
Priya: Agreed. Sam, write it up as ADR-001 and own the key rotation. Monthly rotation to start.
Sam: Will do.
