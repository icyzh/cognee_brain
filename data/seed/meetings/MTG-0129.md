Nora: Goal for this quarter is reliable receipts. Right now we lose some whenever notify has a bad day.
Mei: Because payments calls notify synchronously. If notify is slow, captures are slow; if it's down, the receipt is just gone.
Priya: Payments should never wait on notifications. Can we put a queue in between?
Mei: SQS. Payments publishes captured and refunded events after commit, notify consumes and retries. Dead-letter queue for failures.
Arjun: Why not Kafka? It'd scale better if we grow.
Mei: Nobody here runs Kafka, and our volume is tiny. SQS is managed and we're on AWS already.
Priya: Agree. SQS is ADR-002. Mei owns it.
Nora: Second question: channels. Merchants only asked for email receipts so far.
Mei: Then email only through SES for now. One channel is all I can build well this quarter.
Arjun: SMS will come up eventually.
Nora: Then we revisit. Email-only via SES is ADR-005, Mei owns that too.
