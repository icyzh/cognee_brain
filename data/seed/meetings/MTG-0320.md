Nora: Three of our largest merchants now require SMS receipts. One also wants push through their own app.
Mei: Email-only was the decision in January, ADR-005. It was right for then, not now.
Arjun: Refund confirmations by email barely get opened. Support keeps getting "where's my refund" tickets.
Nora: So the proposal is multi-channel: email, SMS and push, configured per merchant and per event type.
Mei: Keep SES for email since it works. Add an SMS provider, then push. The SQS events stay exactly as they are.
Arjun: Why not move everything to one provider that does all three?
Mei: Migrating email gives us nothing right now, and it's risk.
Nora: SMS costs per message. We pass that through to merchants.
Mei: I'll need per-channel retries and a preference store. SMS first, that's TCK-147.
Nora: Decision: ADR-009, multi-channel notifications, superseding ADR-005. I own it, Mei builds it.
