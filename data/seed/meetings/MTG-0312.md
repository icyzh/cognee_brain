Priya: Main topic is ledger storage. The TCK-131 postmortem is clear: two concurrent captures and a refund on one payment, both refunds went through. Double refund.
Marco: The refund updates the payment and writes two ledger entries. Those are separate documents in Mongo, and our application-level locking missed the race.
Priya: We need multi-document ACID across ledger entries, every time, not most of the time. Postgres gives us serializable transactions and a constraint so balances can't go negative.
Arjun: Mongo has multi-document transactions now, doesn't it?
Marco: We tried them in the incident review. Under our contention they were slow and the retries got messy. I wrote ADR-003 and I think it was right then, but it's the wrong store for a ledger now. I agree with Priya.
Arjun: What's the migration downtime? Merchants will notice if captures stop.
Priya: A short write freeze, minutes not hours, in the Sunday low-traffic window. We backfill first, then freeze, sync the delta and switch.
Arjun: And the reports replica?
Priya: Becomes a native Postgres replica, which makes Lena's life easier.
Marco: I'll help with the backfill scripts.
Priya: Decision: ADR-007, Postgres for the payments ledger, superseding ADR-003. I own it, migration is TCK-142.
