Priya: Two reliability items: merchant retries and the fallout from TCK-131.
Marco: Merchants retry on timeouts and on the new 429s. Right now a retried capture or refund can run twice.
Arjun: The SDKs retry automatically, so this will only get worse as merchants upgrade.
Marco: Proposal: require an Idempotency-Key header on every write. We store the key and the response for 24 hours and replay the response on a repeat.
Arjun: Could we just hash the request body instead?
Marco: Two legitimate identical payments would collide. The key has to come from the client.
Priya: And a repeat key with a different body?
Marco: Reject with 422.
Priya: Good. To be clear, this fixes duplicate requests, not two different requests racing on the same payment. That's what actually happened in TCK-131, and it's a storage problem.
Marco: Agreed, let's take storage next week.
Priya: Idempotency keys are ADR-008, Marco owns it.
