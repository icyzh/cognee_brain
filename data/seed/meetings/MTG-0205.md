title: "Reporting architecture"
attendees: [lena, diego, priya]

---

Lena: Month-end reports for the big merchants scan the live ledger. Twice in January that slowed down captures.
Priya: That can't happen again. Reports can't touch the payment path.
Diego: Finance also wants SQL for their exports. They're tired of waiting on us for CSVs.
Lena: Proposal: a Postgres read replica of the ledger data, loaded nightly. Reports only read from the replica.
Priya: What about streaming it into a warehouse instead?
Diego: Fresher, but it's a lot of infrastructure for two people. Nobody has asked for intraday reports.
Priya: And merchants are fine with yesterday's numbers?
Lena: Nora checked with the top ten. They are.
Diego: The risk is timing. The nightly load has to finish before the report jobs kick off.
Lena: We'll monitor it. Decision is ADR-004, Data owns the replica and the jobs.
Priya: Agreed.
